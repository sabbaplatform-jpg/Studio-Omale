Manifesto

read it out loud

You probably want to know why this Studio exists. You probably want the full story told to you the way how all founding stories are told.

But we don’t have the desire in us to tell it that way, so we choose not to. You see, conformity, following the status quo, has nothing to do with us. We are the contrarians, the rebels, the living fish; swimming against the current, shooting left and whizzing right, and flowing when we want to. Do you get it? It’s all about what we want to do and how we want to do it. So, when the world chooses to replicate and churn out uniformity, we will choose to be abstract, diverse, different. Because whether we like it or not, and even if we try to hide, we will stick out like a sore thumb.

Look, we are those who see the world different, okay? We are those who see the magic in the mundane. We are those who hear music in our dreams and visualise sounds. We are those you might consider neurodivergent. But it is this divergence that gave birth to this studio — the house of unorthodox storytelling, where ideas come from the heart and not from a machine.

Peony BombsImagine a world where bombs are made of flower petals.Read it

Imagine a world where bombs are made of flower petals. Imagine when these bombs would explode and all the pretty peony petals they keep cascade from above raining down pink. And kids will come running out to play and giggle. And their parents will look on and know that these gentle petals will cause no harm but fall with tender embraces. Imagine the cobblestones covered with it — peony carpets. They will hide shrapnel shells and all the other sharp things of war, of the other world where your imagination has yet to sip out and make itself the new reality. Imagine all of this. Those faithful peonies that cover ugliness and make reality worth living in. And hushes nature’s murderous roar so that we can hear the melodies of youth. Those love-filled peonies that mend broken hearts — kintsugi peonies — pure peonies — perfect peonies. If only we would ever give them a chance in this divided world.

I will turn my imagination off now and face the truth, and I will advise you to do the same. The truth I’ve been letting pass these last couple of months. Heartbreaking images that make themselves so common to the point where they are empty of emotion. And although they are empty, I see children. Children empty of life — motionless children. What would happen to dull the dance of youth? I stare at them and my heart breaks, but my mouth cannot utter a thing. And my eyes would well up, but it will not spill. What has become of me? Only now have they broken, listening to the tender voice of Radka Toneff singing “The Moon’s a Harsh Mistress”. But what has the moon got to do with this? The moon so far, so lofty, begotten of harvest glory, voluptuous in Victorian vigour — what has she got to do with it? I lay listening. My heart festered in anguish. I will write more about this when I am able to process it. I will speak about it when I’m unmuted. I will because it’s the right thing to do.

The ones who burnSome people just have that spark, that thing, that flare, elegance, warmth, pizzaz.Read it

Some people just have that spark, that thing, that flare, elegance, warmth, pizzaz. And they’ll come dashing through, crashing through like a meteorite with only one purpose of colliding and leaving an impact. You will see them from across the street and know who they are. That light, lustre, glow they produce which reflects their soul, their internal reality, will leave you in a trance-like state, and all the universe will pause in that very moment to allow them to walk by.

These people with their visible flame, set alight the wick of palsied nights aflame, burning, burning, until day springs forth out of sterile tomorrow, beckoning a new story. If you dare come too close to these people, they will suck you in with that magnetic allure that they have. You will find some sort of solace being nearby. And the effort you once abused to flow, to harmonise, will be useless. You don’t need to fan the flame with these people. They are just burning, it is what they do, it is what they are.

Where do these light people come from anyway? Where do they hide in the splendour of our cities? Why can’t we find them under the pristine skyscrapers, the rushing trains hurtling hundreds, thousands, millions of people from station to station? Why can’t we find them on the countryside moors where biblical donkeys still stand, hee-hawing everlasting mysteries with their sad eyes, sad ears, sad hooves, sad mouths? Surely they have another realm of existence and only cross in and out of ours to remind us that they do exist.

How do we conjure them out of this realm? How do we keep them in our realm? Is it ablution? Is it a sacred dance? Is it psalms? Is it love songs? Is it a prayer crafted tenderly in the inner chambers of our heart, held out like an Abrahamic lamb on the altar of stone, waiting for a thunderbolt, waiting for a flame, waiting for a smoke to descend? What is that thing we need to do to keep them here?

We do not know. So we’ll go about the ancient labyrinths of our lives, catching the eyes of strangers, wanderers, and we’ll fall in love again and again, seeking the eternal flame we once saw in these light people. We’ll go about in the darkness of our lives, reaching out for the hem of a coat, a shoulder to lean on, a hand to hold. We’ll go about searching every crevice, turning every stone, opening every door hoping that we’ll find them on the other side. We’ll go about, because that is all we’ll end up knowing, that is all we’ll have to do, and this great earth of ours will continue to spin, continue to dance around the sun, continue to be.

What I do know, is that if you find these people, those with that spark, that childlike youth, that insatiable appetite for life, that easiness of existence, keep them. I don’t know what you need to do to keep them, but you better go and find out.

Tabula RasaWipe the slate clean. Begin anew again.Read it

Wipe the slate clean. Begin anew again. On the scuffs and remnants of the old you will paint new worlds. You do not have to take yourself to the wilderness, nor will you have to go up the mountain top, and neither will you have to holla until an answer is received. There will be no ablution upon ablution, no fasting until the spirit and the flesh separate, and neither will there be other types of self-flagellations. Instead, you will happen. You will happen like the sun does every day, with no need to whimper for assistance. You will flourish like purple heathers that lie bountiful on the English moors. You will flow and go the way of your lifeline without resistance, and when you do – man, what a day that will be. To see your luminescent face pouring out love and fulfilment. To hear of all the magnificent things you’ve been up to and to know that all of these have come about because you decided to happen – to give of yourself. For what do you know of another to give of them? Are you not like the acorn, which by happening, continues to unravel until it becomes the Oak tree? Go on. Check it. Search through your family albums. You will find this Oak tree unravelling. Has it changed? In that acorn, that embryo, all of life’s happenings have been encoded, waiting to be played. And of course, when the stylus hits the groove, there will be noise. And when the speakers amplify the music, it might be muffled by the sound of others – you will not be heard, and you will go unseen for a time. But there will come a jolting of the deck, which will cause the stylus to skip, and that will awaken you to reality. With this, you will find your voice in the crowd, and as you focus on your voice, it will amplify. It will reverberate the walls of your imprisonment, and those walls will come down so you can happen. So, continue. Do not look at others for assurance. You know what to do. It’s in you. Written in your DNA. This iteration of yourself is on a particular mission, and if you let it unravel, it will not benefit you alone, but others too – just like the oak tree does.
